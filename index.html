Since 2002

A taste of home, served with pride.

Mexico Tipico began in 2002, when a young couple turned a dream into a family restaurant rooted in humble hospitality and authentic Mexican cuisine.

Inspired by a childhood in Jalisco and lessons learned in a U.S. restaurant kitchen, the owners built Mexico Tipico around one simple idea: even when life gets busy, there should always be time to slow down and enjoy home-cooked food.
